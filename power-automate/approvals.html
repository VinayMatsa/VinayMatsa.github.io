Approvals

VM Vinay Matsa 21 November 2026 3 min read

The Approvals connector adds a human decision to a flow. Start and wait for an approval sends a request (email, Teams, Approvals centre), pauses the flow, and continues when someone responds.

How an approval works

WhatA step that pauses the flow until approvers respond, then returns the outcome and comments.
WhyMany processes need a person's yes or no before the next step.
DemoApprove a new laptop for the design team (1,450).

Reading the result

body('Start_and_wait_for_an_approval')?['outcome']
PieceWhat it does
?['outcome']Approve or Reject (or a custom response).
coalesce(first(body('Start_and_wait_for_an_approval')?['responses'])?['comments'], 'No comment')
PieceWhat it does
?['responses']One entry per approver who answered.
first(...)?['comments']The first approver's comment.
coalesce(..., 'No comment')Fallback when none was given.

Approval types

TypeFinishes when
Approve/Reject - First to respondAny one approver responds
Approve/Reject - Everyone must approveAll approve, or anyone rejects
Custom responsesSomeone picks one of your buttons (e.g. Need more info)
Sequential approvalEach approver in turn

Timeouts and approvers from a list

When nobody responds

Approval action → Settings → Timeout P7D (7 days)
Next step: Configure run after → has timed out
Set status Expired, tell the requester

Approvers from data, not hard-coded

Get items from Approvers: Product eq 'Laptops'
Select: item()?['ApproverEmail']
Assigned to: join(body('Select'), ';')

When an approver changes, the business edits the list; the flow isn't touched.

When a policy blocks it

This is an admin data policy (an advanced connector policy that allows only listed connectors). Nothing in the flow is wrong; the environment doesn't allow the connector. Check allowed connectors before you build.

Common mistakes

  • No timeout, so runs hang for 30 days and then fail.
  • Approvers hard-coded in the flow.
  • Reading comments without a fallback.
  • Discovering a blocked connector after the build.
