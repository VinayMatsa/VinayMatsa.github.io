Data loss prevention (DLP) policies

VM Vinay Matsa 21 November 2026 3 min read

Admins use data policies (DLP policies) to stop company data flowing where it shouldn't. A policy decides, per environment, which connectors are allowed and which may be used together.

What it looks like

WhatAdmin rules per environment: connector groups, or an allowlist.
WhyA flow can be perfect and still refuse to turn on. Knowing why saves hours.
DemoThe Approvals connector blocked in the demo environment.

New connectors land in the default group (Non-Business unless the admin changes it).

Advanced connector policies

The error above names an Advanced Connector Policy: a strict allowlist. Every connector that isn't explicitly allowed is blocked, so a connector that works in one environment can be refused in another.

Common mistakes

  • Rebuilding a flow that a policy blocks.
  • Developing with looser rules than production.
  • Mixing Business and Non-Business connectors.
