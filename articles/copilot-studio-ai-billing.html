AI billing in Copilot Studio: tokens, credits and limits

VM Vinay Matsa 14 October 2026 2 min read

Every answer, action and reasoning step an agent performs consumes Copilot Credits. But the harness, the user's licence and the channel decide whether that usage is actually billed.

Start from the root

  • A token is a small piece of text the AI reads or writes, roughly a short word or part of one.
  • A Copilot Credit is the unit Microsoft charges for AI work in Copilot Studio.
  • You never pay in tokens. Agent activity is priced in credits per type of event.

One question, priced step by step

Using the published rates at the time of writing (October 2026):

StepFeatureCredits
Search the HR policyTenant graph grounding10
Write the answerGenerative answer2
Check the balance in the HR systemAgent action5
Total for one question17

Who pays?

  • Users licensed for Microsoft 365 Copilot, in Microsoft channels: included, within fair use.
  • Unlicensed users and external websites: billed.
  • The harness matters too: some harnesses bill even while you build and test. See harnesses in Copilot Studio.

Ways to buy

  • Pay-as-you-go: billed per credit through an Azure subscription ($0.01 per credit at the time of writing).
  • Capacity packs: a fixed number of credits per month (25,000 for $200 at the time of writing).
  • Pre-purchase plans for a year, with a discount.

Keeping costs under control

  • Unused capacity-pack credits expire each month.
  • An Azure budget only alerts; it never stops usage.
  • Custom agents are disabled when usage reaches 125% of prepaid capacity.
  • Set a monthly limit per agent in the admin center, so one busy agent can't use everyone's capacity.
Tip: prices and rates change. Before a business case, check the current billing rates page and the licensing guide.
