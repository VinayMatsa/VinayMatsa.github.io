Flow ownership and service accounts

VM Vinay Matsa 21 November 2026 3 min read

Every flow has an owner. Automated and scheduled flows run with the owner's licence and allowance, and their connections usually belong to the owner. If that person leaves, the flow can stop.

Owners, co-owners and run-only users

WhatRoles that decide who can edit, run and support a flow, and whose account it depends on.
WhyA flow tied to one person stops when that person's account changes.
DemoMoving a production flow to a service account with a support team as co-owners.
RoleCan do
OwnerEverything
Co-ownerEdit, run history, settings, connections, add owners; can't remove the creator
Run-only userStart an instant flow with their own or the owner's connections

Common mistakes

  • A critical flow owned by one person.
  • Changing the owner but leaving connections on the old account.
  • Sharing the service account password widely.
