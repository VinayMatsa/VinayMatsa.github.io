One solution, every component

WhatA solution is a container that carries tables, the app, flows, the agent, prompts, environment variables and connection references together.
WhyMoving parts one by one breaks the links between them. A solution moves them as a set.
DemoCustomerCareAgent_1_0_0_1.zip, imported with Solutions → Import.
