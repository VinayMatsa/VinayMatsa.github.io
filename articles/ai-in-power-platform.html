AI in Power Platform: models, generative AI and agents

VM Vinay Matsa 26 May 2026 2 min read

Choosing the right AI capability is key to building effective solutions in Power Platform. Think of it as a simple progression: models, prompts, agents.

Start from the root

"AI" in Power Platform covers three quite different things:

  • A model does one trained task with structured output, like reading an invoice into fields.
  • A prompt asks a large language model to understand or write text, like summarising an email.
  • An agent holds a conversation, decides what to do and calls tools to do it.

Which tool for which job

CapabilityBest forUse when
AI Builder modelsDocument and invoice extraction, OCR, classification, prediction from business dataYou need reliable, structured output
Prompts (generative AI)Summaries, drafting, extracting meaning from unstructured textYou're working with natural language
Copilot Studio agentsChat experiences that answer and act through flows and connectorsYou need AI that can both reason and act

How they work together

An agent can use a prompt for reasoning, call an AI Builder model for extraction, and trigger a flow for the action. Take invoice processing:

  1. An invoice is uploaded; an AI Builder model extracts the fields.
  2. A prompt validates or formats the text.
  3. A flow starts the approval and notifies the requester, and an agent lets people ask about status.

The Learn section on this site shows these pieces in flows: AI Builder document processing and prompts with clean JSON output.

The real power isn't in one tool but in combining models, prompts and agents, each doing what it's best at.
